# Rutu — A Digital Love Letter & Personal Cinematic Birthday Website

A custom-crafted, personal cinematic experience and interactive love letter created specifically for **Sanskriti (“Rutu”)** from **Hassan**.

---

## 🌟 Overview & Narrative Structure

The website guides Rutu through a 13-Act emotional arc:

1. **Opening Screen**: Minimal midnight introduction — *"For someone who deserves to slow down for a minute."* &bull; **RUTU** &bull; *"Come in &rarr;"*
2. **Act I (Hero)**: Full-screen rooftop portrait with cinematic grain & vignette &bull; *"Happy Birthday, Rutu. Before anything else… I want you to know how proud I am of you."*
3. **Act II (I See You)**: Celebrating her quiet ambition, discipline, and late-night study sessions &bull; *"Your ambition is one of the things I love most about you."*
4. **Act III (But Rutu…)**: Shifting into warm ivory tones &bull; *"You don't always have to be productive... Sometimes, just live."* Floating organic words (*breathe, laugh, wander, eat, dance, sleep, travel, do nothing*).
5. **Act IV (Take a Breath)**: Interactive glowing gradient orb &bull; Press & hold to inhale, release to exhale &bull; *"See? Life is allowed to feel this slow sometimes."*
6. **Act V (Memory Gallery)**: Horizontal scrolling filmstrip with candid Polaroid photos and motion video loops with handwritten captions (*"this one.", "you were cute here.", "I still remember this.", "one of my favorites."*).
7. **Act VI (The Trust)**: Intimate, private conversation &bull; *"Every time you trust me with another little piece of your world… somehow, I fall for you a little harder."*
8. **Act VII (When Life Gets Hard)**: Safe harbor promises &bull; Kinetic typography docking animation where the word **"Beside"** smoothly slides and docks next to **"you"**.
9. **Act VIII (You Won)**: Deep midnight to warm golden aura &bull; *"You won because you became someone you can be proud of... And somehow, I think I won too, because I got to meet you."*
10. **Act IX (Horizons & Future)**: Twinkling starfield constellation grid of all the unwalked roads, unreached dreams, and peaceful mornings waiting for her, accompanied by her radiant twirl video loop.
11. **Act X (I Want You to Live)**: Philosophical heart &bull; *"I don't just want you to succeed. I want you to live... And while you're chasing all of it, I hope I get to be there."*
12. **Act XI (Personal Editorial Letter)**: Warm paper editorial letter written by Hassan celebrating Rutu's heart, vulnerability, and dreams.
13. **Act XII (Sunrise Finale)**: Sky transforms into dawn colors &bull; *"Go live your life. Study hard. Dream ridiculously... Happy Birthday, Rutu. — Hassan"*
14. **Act XIII (Interactive Birthday Candle)**: Minimal dark sanctuary &bull; Click to blow out the flickering birthday candle &bull; Flame extinguishes with rising smoke particles &bull; Sky opens into glowing starlight memories &bull; *"I hope this year is kind to you. And I hope you let yourself enjoy it. ♥ Hassan & Rutu"*

## 🎵 Soundtrack & Video Features

- **Ambient Soundtrack**: Toggle button on top-right plays the soothing music from `photos/lv_7680106392031726856_20260903182956.mp4` (*"Pehle Bhi Main"* / September Montage) with generative Web Audio fallback.
- **Our Reel Button**: Opens the birthday video reel overlay with a single click.
- **Interactive Cursor**: Dynamic desktop custom cursor with contextual labels (`look`, `hold`, `breathe`, `wish`, `open`).

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can run the project with Python:
```bash
python -m http.server 3000
```
Then open your browser and navigate to:
```
http://localhost:3000
```
Or simply double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome / Edge / Safari.
